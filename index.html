04 / THE PRACTICAL DETAILS

Good questions.
Straight answers.

What is included in the starting price?+

The $599 starting price covers a focused website with up to 5 core pages, mobile-responsive design, a basic contact form, and two pre-launch revision rounds. Hosting and standard technical maintenance are $99/month. Optional functionality is quoted separately before work begins.

Can I add booking, payments, or an online store?+

Yes. Chatbots, payment forms, scheduling, ecommerce, advanced forms, and business integrations can be scoped for your business. The implementation price and any ongoing software, processing, usage, or additional support costs will be identified in your proposal.

What does the $99/month cover?+

Hosting, SSL, uptime monitoring, scheduled backups, routine technical maintenance, and fixes for defects in the delivered website. It is not a content-editing or redesign subscription.

Can I request changes after launch?+

Yes. Send the changes you need and I’ll provide a separate quote before starting. Additional pages, new features, and content changes are not included in monthly maintenance.

How long will my website take?+

We’ll agree on a realistic schedule after reviewing your pages, content, and requirements. Your timeline depends on receiving the needed materials and feedback.

Will I need to supply content?+

The base package assumes you provide your business information, approved copy, brand assets, and images you have rights to use. Copywriting and image sourcing can be discussed separately.

What about ownership and cancellation?+

Ownership, billing start date, any minimum term, domain costs, and migration arrangements will be documented in your proposal before you commit.

Are the projects above real client work?+

They are illustrative design concepts, not live client websites or claims of client results.

Can you help beyond web design?+

Branding, social media, local SEO, graphic design, and digital marketing can be discussed and quoted separately.
