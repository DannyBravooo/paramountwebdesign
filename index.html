FAQ

Questions we get a lot

How does the pricing work?+
Simple and two-part: a one-time $1,000 build fee covers the custom design, build, and launch of your website. After that, $99 a month covers hosting, security, backups, ongoing edits and updates, and a monthly check-in. No surprise invoices, ever.
What does the $99/month care plan cover?+
Hosting, security, backups, and fixes — plus new pages, photos, offers, and edits as your business changes, and a monthly review of how your site looks, loads, and performs. A website isn't a one-off purchase; it needs someone watching it, and that's exactly what the care plan takes care of.
How long does a website take to build?+
It depends on the scope, and we'll give you a clear timeline in your proposal before anything starts. Most small business sites come together in a few weeks once we have your content and photos.
Do I own my website?+
You paid for the build, so the site is built for you and your business. Your care plan keeps it hosted, secure, and up to date — and if you ever want to talk about changing or moving it, we'll work it out with you. No games.
Do you only work with Boston businesses?+
We're Boston-born and proud of it — but we work with small businesses anywhere. Local clients get the in-person treatment; everyone else gets the same quality, just over video calls.
Can you help with branding, social media, or SEO too?+
Yes — that's the point of having six services under one roof. Branding, social media management, local SEO, graphic design, and digital marketing are all available, and bundling them with your website saves you money.
